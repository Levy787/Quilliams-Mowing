# Social Rebrand Direction

The current social presence reads as a practical home-service page. That is useful for trust, but the daily facts series needs a more editorial layer so the accounts do not feel like adverts.

The goal is not to hide the business. The goal is to make the feed feel like useful garden knowledge from a local expert.

## Recommended Position

Primary brand:

> Quilliams Gardening & Landscaping

Editorial series:

> Garden Fact of the Day

One-line profile idea:

> Daily facts about lawns, soil, plants, fungi, and garden life from a Cornwall gardener.

## Profile Updates

### Facebook

Current page name in the screenshot:

> Quilliams Mowing & Hedge Trimming

Recommended display:

> Quilliams Gardening & Landscaping

Cover image direction:

> Cornish Garden Notes
> Lawns, soil, plants, fungi & garden life

Avoid putting the phone number and email as the dominant cover message if the feed is becoming education-led. Those details can stay in the page info and button.

### Instagram

Current display feels very narrow:

> Quilliams Mowing

Recommended display:

> Quilliams Gardening & Landscaping

Bio option:

```text
Daily lawn, soil & garden facts.
Cornwall garden care, explained simply.
Newquay-based.
```

If Instagram bio space is tight:

```text
Daily lawn, soil & garden facts.
Cornwall gardens, explained simply.
```

## Visual System

Use a repeatable card system so the posts become recognizable.

### Format

- Size: `1080x1350`
- Background: macro garden image
- Overlay: soft dark green / charcoal gradient
- Top label: `GARDEN FACT OF THE DAY`
- Main text: one strong fact
- Bottom line: `Quilliams Gardening & Landscaping`
- Optional footer CTA: `Follow for daily facts about lawns, soil, and garden life.`

### Colour Direction

- Deep leaf green
- Off-white
- Soil brown as an accent
- Pale lichen green as a highlight

Avoid high-saturation service-ad green on every post. It makes the content look like a flyer.

### Typography Direction

- Bold, clear headline face for the fact.
- Smaller editorial serif or simple sans for supporting text.
- Keep facts short enough to read in 1-2 seconds.

## Content Voice

The tone should be:

- curious
- calm
- practical
- specific
- not salesy

Avoid:

- "Book now"
- "Need help?"
- "Contact us today"
- "We can fix this"
- stock motivational garden captions

Use:

- "Moss is a symptom, not the villain."
- "Soil is not dirt. Soil is a living system."
- "A mushroom is only the visible part."
- "Short grass is stressed grass."

## Why This Helps Growth

Pure education builds remembered expertise. People may not book from a single fact post, but after seeing useful facts for weeks, they know the page belongs to someone who understands gardens rather than just selling garden work.

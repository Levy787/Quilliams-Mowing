# SEO Audit: quilliamsmowing.co.uk

Date: 2026-05-04
Site: https://quilliamsmowing.co.uk/
Business type: Local service business, gardening and landscaping, Cornwall
Audit basis: live crawl, sitemap crawl, Codex SEO runners, Lighthouse mobile/desktop lab checks, and public search visibility checks.

## Executive Summary

Live SEO health score: 67/100.

The site has a solid local-service foundation: the homepage is indexable, content is server-rendered, all 28 sitemap URLs return 200, SSL/security headers are strong, image alt text coverage is good, and the homepage has useful proof signals such as reviews, project examples, service coverage, phone number, and pricing ranges.

The biggest issue is not the codebase. It is that the live site is still serving an older cached/deployed version. The pushed local commit already contains fixes for several important findings, but the live site has not picked them up. Evidence: the homepage response had an `age` header around 1,005,833 seconds, `/llms.txt` returned 404, `/images/uploads/site/og-image.png` returned 404, `/areas/newquay` returned 404, and live `robots.txt` is still the old minimal file.

Once the deployment/cache mismatch is resolved, the next growth work is content depth and local authority: richer service pages, stronger Newquay/location pages, fresher Google reviews, and more consistent citations.

## Critical Issues

### 1. Live deployment/cache mismatch is hiding the latest SEO fixes

Live still serves the older version of multiple assets/routes:

- `/llms.txt`: 404
- `/images/uploads/site/og-image.png`: 404
- `/areas/newquay`: 404
- `/areas/wadebridge`: 404
- `/areas/st-agnes`: 404
- `/robots.txt`: old minimal file, no admin/path disallows
- homepage schema: old `LocalBusiness` and duplicate review schema
- footer: still says "Designed and Developed by TradeSender", not the committed Quilliam.ai footer

Why it matters: this makes live SEO look worse than the repository, breaks social previews, blocks the new AI-search asset, and leaves the primary Newquay location page absent.

Fix:

1. Verify Vercel deployment for commit `8bfb033`.
2. Purge Cloudflare/Vercel cache.
3. Re-check `/llms.txt`, `/robots.txt`, `/sitemap.xml`, `/areas/newquay`, and the OG image.
4. Resubmit sitemap in Google Search Console after the new sitemap is live.

### 2. Indexed legacy pages return 404 or low-value redirects

Public search shows `/about-us` indexed, but live `/about-us` returns 404. Search also shows old article URLs from `/articles/...`; those currently redirect to the homepage.

Why it matters: 404s and broad homepage redirects waste legacy relevance. The old article URLs have topical gardening content that should either be restored or redirected to closely matching service/content pages.

Fix:

- Add `/about-us -> /about` as a permanent redirect.
- Map old article URLs to the closest relevant service page or rebuild the articles if they brought qualified traffic.
- Monitor GSC "Not found (404)" and "Page with redirect" reports after deployment.

### 3. Canonical and trailing-slash duplication risk

Live pages missing canonical tags include `/services`, `/projects`, `/quote`, `/about`, `/refer`, `/privacy`, and `/terms`. Live also serves both `/services` and `/services/` as 200.

Why it matters: duplicate URL forms split signals and make crawlers choose canonicals instead of being told clearly.

Fix:

- Deploy the local canonical fixes already present in the repo.
- Enforce one URL shape globally, preferably no trailing slash.
- Re-crawl every sitemap URL and its trailing-slash variant.

### 4. CMS/admin route is public

Live `/keystatic` returns 200, and live robots does not disallow it.

Why it matters: admin surfaces should not be indexable or publicly visible unless intentionally protected elsewhere. Even if auth blocks interaction, returning public HTML is not ideal for crawl hygiene.

Fix:

- Require auth before rendering admin content.
- Add `noindex` or block/disallow `/keystatic`.
- Deploy the updated robots rules already committed locally.

### 5. Live local schema has inconsistencies

Live homepage JSON-LD currently has:

- `@type`: `LocalBusiness`, not `LandscapingBusiness`.
- Schema email: `info@quilliamsmowing.co.uk`, while visible/business content uses `levi@quilliamsmowing.co.uk`.
- Schema hours: Monday-Friday 08:00-18:00 and Saturday 09:00-16:00, while footer says Monday-Friday 08:00-17:00 and weekend/bank holidays closed.
- Missing postal code/street address detail.
- Review schema creates a second `LocalBusiness` without the same `@id`.
- Review dates are human readable, e.g. `20 Oct 2025`, not ISO date format.
- `sameAs` only includes Facebook and Instagram live.

Why it matters: NAP/hours consistency is a trust and local SEO signal. Schema inconsistency also weakens entity understanding for search and AI systems.

Fix:

- Deploy the committed schema updates.
- Use one canonical email, phone format, and business-hours policy across schema, footer, contact page, Yell, GBP, and other citations.
- Add `postalCode`, `geo`, `logo`, `image`, `priceRange`, `areaServed`, and richer `sameAs`.

## High Priority

### 6. Primary location page is missing live

The business targets Newquay heavily, but live `/areas/newquay` returns 404 and the sitemap does not list it. This is the single most important location page for a Newquay-based service business.

Fix:

- Deploy `/areas/newquay`.
- Add it to sitemap.
- Link it from the footer, areas index, homepage service area section, and relevant service pages.

### 7. Area pages are thin on live

Live area pages are roughly 243-272 words:

- Truro: 262 words
- St Austell: 272 words
- Bodmin: 255 words
- Padstow: 247 words
- Perranporth: 243 words
- St Ives: 256 words

Quality gate: primary location pages should be 600+ words; secondary locations should be 500+ words with genuine local detail.

Fix:

- Deploy the richer local area content already in `lib/areas/data.ts`.
- Add area-specific proof: local testimonials, photos, project links, neighborhoods, travel/service expectations, common local garden problems.
- Keep the total number of area pages controlled; this is safe at the current scale, but keep uniqueness high.

### 8. Core service pages are below the 800-word service-page gate

Live service pages are mostly under 600 words:

- Garden maintenance: 569
- Hedge trimming: 531
- Lawn care: 549
- Landscaping: 436
- Mulching: 430
- Seasonal cleanup: 434

Why it matters: dedicated service pages are important for local organic and AI visibility. Thin pages can rank, but richer pages usually win more long-tail and conversion queries.

Fix:

- Expand each core service page to 800+ useful words.
- Add pricing ranges, seasonal timing, common Cornwall/Newquay conditions, before/after project links, FAQs, and service-specific internal links.
- Prioritize lawn care, hedge trimming, landscaping, and garden maintenance.

### 9. Title tags are over-long and double-branded

Many live titles exceed 60 characters or repeat brand terms:

- `/services`: 78 chars, includes `Quilliams | Quilliams Gardening`.
- `/contact`: 73 chars, includes `Quilliams Gardening | Quilliams Gardening`.
- Service pages: often 79-86 chars.
- Area pages: often 76-82 chars.

Fix:

- Keep titles around 50-60 characters.
- Avoid manually adding the brand when the layout template appends it.
- Example: `Hedge Trimming in Newquay & Cornwall | Quilliams`.

### 10. Meta descriptions are weak on support/conversion pages

Several descriptions are too short or generic:

- `/privacy`: 50 chars
- `/terms`: 33 chars
- `/site-map`: 62 chars
- `/refer`: 93 chars
- `/contact`: 107 chars
- `/pricing`: 110 chars

Fix:

- Write unique 120-160 character descriptions.
- For low-value legal pages, this is not urgent; for contact/pricing/refer it is worth improving.

### 11. Mobile performance needs work

Clean mobile Lighthouse lab run:

- Performance: 54
- LCP: 4.5s
- TBT: 2,480ms
- TTI: 10.5s
- CLS: 0
- Total transfer: 1,766 KiB

Main contributors:

- Cloudflare Turnstile/challenge assets.
- PostHog survey/recording/autocapture scripts.
- Heavy hero/service images.
- Unused JS, estimated 144 KiB.

Fix:

- Load Turnstile only on forms/routes where needed.
- Disable PostHog surveys/session recording/dead-click capture on public landing pages unless actively needed.
- Reduce homepage client JS, especially animated/duplicated hero image loops.
- Tune Next image `sizes`, avoid pulling 640/750px images for smaller rendered dimensions.

### 12. Accessibility contrast failures affect UX and indirectly SEO

Lighthouse flags contrast failures on the green banner/CTA elements:

- `Call: 07593 121 621`
- `Get a Free Quote Today`
- banner location/offer text

Fix:

- Darken the green or use darker text/stronger contrast.
- Re-test mobile and desktop contrast after deploying.

## Medium Priority

### 13. Sitemap is valid but stale

The sitemap is clean in status coverage but weak in freshness:

- 28 URLs, all 200.
- All `lastmod` values identical.
- Uses `changefreq` and `priority`, which Google ignores.
- Missing newly committed area pages if they are meant to be live.

Fix:

- Generate `lastmod` from content file modified dates or explicit content metadata.
- Remove `changefreq` and `priority` unless needed for non-Google consumers.
- Ensure sitemap updates after deployment.

### 14. AI search readiness is moderate, not strong

Codex GEO score: 75.

Strengths:

- AI crawlers are effectively allowed because robots allows all.
- Structured data exists.
- Homepage has some citation-worthy passages and proof points.

Weaknesses:

- No live `/llms.txt`.
- Weak author/founder/date attribution in visible page content.
- Few answer-first, data-rich blocks.
- Citation ecosystem is still light.

Fix:

- Deploy `/llms.txt`.
- Add visible founder/reviewer/update signals.
- Add direct, citable FAQ-style answers with numbers, locations, service ranges, and constraints.
- Build high-authority citations and social/video proof.

### 15. Image SEO is good on alt text but weak on delivery

Codex image score: 68.

Strengths:

- 36 homepage images.
- 0 missing alt attributes.
- 0 weak alt attributes detected.

Weaknesses:

- 12 images missing width/height attributes.
- 4 sampled below-fold images not lazy loaded.
- Mobile Lighthouse estimated 244 KiB image savings.
- Old live decorative `/patterns/pattern-2.svg` is still being served at about 69 KiB.

Fix:

- Deploy the committed PNG pattern replacement.
- Add stable dimensions/aspect ratios.
- Tune `sizes` and compression for carousel/service imagery.
- Keep only true LCP/above-fold images high priority.

### 16. FAQ schema should not be treated as a Google rich-result tactic

FAQPage exists on a commercial local service site. This is valid structured data, but Google FAQ rich results are now restricted mostly to government and health contexts.

Fix:

- Keep FAQs for users and AI answer extraction.
- Do not expect FAQ rich result wins from this markup.
- Consider marking up page/entity data with `WebSite`, `WebPage`, `Service`, `BreadcrumbList`, and strong `LocalBusiness` instead.

### 17. Review freshness and third-party review distribution are weak

Homepage shows 16 reviews and a 5.0 aggregate rating. The latest visible review is December 12, 2025, which is stale as of 2026-05-04. Yell listing was found but shows 0 ratings in the public result.

Fix:

- Build a compliant post-job review request flow.
- Target 2-3 new reviews per month.
- Respond to every Google review.
- Add review presence on Yell, Checkatrade, Bark, Facebook, and ideally Apple/Bing-adjacent sources.

### 18. Citation/NAP consistency needs tightening

Public results show Yell, Companies House/GOV.UK, and the site. There are possible entity/address inconsistencies:

- Site schema: Newquay/Cornwall without full street/postal details.
- Yell result: "This One Beacon Rd, Summercourt, Newquay, TR8 5DN".
- Companies House result: Pembroke, Dyfed registered office.

Some mismatch is normal for registered office vs service area, but citation profiles should be deliberate and consistent.

Fix:

- Define public NAP policy: service-area address visibility, phone, email, business name.
- Align GBP, Yell, Checkatrade, Bark, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Facebook, and website schema.
- If hiding street address as a service-area business, ensure GBP and schema strategy reflect that.

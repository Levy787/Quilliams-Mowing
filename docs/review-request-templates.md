# Review Request Templates

Use these templates to request Google reviews from happy customers. Send via text or email after completing a job.

## SMS Template (Recommended)

Best sent same day or next day after job completion:

```
Hi [NAME]! 👋

Thanks for choosing Quilliams Gardening. Hope you're happy with how the garden looks!

If you have 30 seconds, a Google review would really help us out:
[GOOGLE_REVIEW_LINK]

No worries if not — thanks again!

Levi
```

## Email Template

```
Subject: Quick favour? ⭐

Hi [NAME],

Thanks again for having us round to sort the garden — hope you're pleased with how it turned out!

I've got a small favour to ask: if you have 30 seconds, would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? It makes a huge difference for a small local business like ours.

👉 [GOOGLE_REVIEW_LINK]

Even just a sentence or two helps. No pressure at all if you're busy!

Thanks,
Levi
Quilliams Gardening
```

## Follow-Up (if no response after 1 week)

```
Hi [NAME], just a gentle nudge — if you had a good experience, a quick Google review would mean a lot! [GOOGLE_REVIEW_LINK] 🙏
```

---

## Your Google Review Link

To get your direct review link:

1. Search "Quilliams Mowing" on Google
2. Click your business listing
3. Click "Get more reviews" button
4. Copy the link

Or use this format:
```
https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=YOUR_PLACE_ID
```

---

## Tips for More Reviews

1. **Timing matters** — Ask within 24 hours while the job is fresh
2. **Text > Email** — SMS gets 3x higher response rate
3. **Make it easy** — Always include the direct link
4. **Be personal** — Use their name, mention the specific work
5. **Don't incentivize** — Google prohibits offering discounts for reviews
6. **Follow up once** — A gentle reminder is fine, don't spam
